Saktah

Practise reciting the Qur'an one breath at a time. Your webcam notices when you take a breath in the middle of a phrase, so you learn to breathe only where a stop is allowed.

Before the camera turns on

  • What it watches. Your face and upper chest, to follow your breathing and whether your lips are moving. No microphone is used.
  • Where it is processed. On this computer, inside the SmartSpectra SDK. No video, image or breathing reading is sent anywhere.
  • What goes online. Only the SDK's sign-in check of the developer key. SDK usage telemetry is switched off in Saktah.
  • What is kept. Nothing. Results live in memory and are gone when you close the app. Stop the camera at any time with the Stop button or by closing the window.
  • What it is not. A recitation aid, not a health or medical tool. It makes no claims about your body.

Kept in memory for this session only.
